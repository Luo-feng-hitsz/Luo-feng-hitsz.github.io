我很乐意交流研究想法、工程实践与潜在合作。如果你对相关方向感兴趣，欢迎通过 [Email](mailto:hitszluofeng@foxmail.com) 或 [GitHub](https://github.com/Luo-feng-hitsz) 联系我。

## About Me

Hi, I am **Luo Feng**, a second-year master's student in Computer Technology at [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. [Cuiyun Gao](https://cuiyungao.github.io/). I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from the same university.

My research focuses on **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)**, with particular interests in LLM-based code generation, C-to-Rust translation, reinforcement learning, and the evaluation and optimization of software engineering agents. I have published two first-author papers at **ASE 2026** and **ICSME 2025**.

Beyond academic research, I enjoy turning research ideas into practical systems. My recent work includes intelligent customer-service agents, repository-level code migration agents, and observability and evaluation pipelines for agentic systems. My goal is to build AI-powered software engineering tools that are reliable, measurable, and useful in real-world development workflows.

I am always open to research discussions, engineering exchanges, and potential collaborations. Please feel free to contact me via [Email](mailto:hitszluofeng@foxmail.com) or [GitHub](https://github.com/Luo-feng-hitsz).
